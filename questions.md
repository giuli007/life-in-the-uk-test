# Questions from the life in the uk test that we got in our tests

All but one question were straighforward (not trick or similar answers that gave way to confusion).

All questions were asked in a similar way on the mock exams of this site: [Life In the UK Test Web | Exams](https://lifeintheuktestweb.co.uk/exams/) and on the official life in the uk app [Official Life in the UK test app | Apple Store](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/official-life-in-the-uk-test/id632064672)
